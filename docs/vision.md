# Project Vision

## Background

The rapid development of quantum computing introduces new challenges to modern cybersecurity.

Current digital systems rely heavily on traditional public-key cryptographic algorithms, such as RSA and ECC, to protect sensitive information and secure communication.

Although these algorithms remain secure against classical computers, large-scale quantum computers may threaten their security through quantum algorithms such as Shor's algorithm.

Therefore, organizations need to understand potential cryptographic risks and prepare for the transition toward Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC).

---

## Problem Statement

Many organizations and users are not aware of how quantum computing may affect their existing security infrastructure.

Current challenges include:

- Lack of awareness regarding quantum threats
- Difficulty identifying vulnerable cryptographic algorithms
- Lack of practical guidance for PQC migration
- Complexity of understanding cryptographic security risks

Without proper preparation, systems using vulnerable cryptographic algorithms may face security risks in the future quantum era.

---

## Vision

QuantumShield AI aims to build an intelligent Post-Quantum Security Analysis Platform that helps users identify cryptographic risks, understand quantum threats, and prepare for future security migration.

The platform combines cybersecurity analysis, artificial intelligence, and Post-Quantum Cryptography to provide practical security assessment and recommendations.

---

## Objectives

The objectives of QuantumShield AI are:

1. Develop an automated cryptographic security analysis system.

2. Identify cryptographic algorithms that may be vulnerable to quantum attacks.

3. Provide recommendations for adopting Post-Quantum Cryptography.

4. Use AI agents to explain security analysis results and provide user-friendly guidance.

5. Build an extensible security platform for future research and development.

---

## Target Users

QuantumShield AI is designed for:

- Developers who need to evaluate cryptographic usage
- Security professionals performing security assessments
- Researchers exploring Post-Quantum Cryptography
- Organizations preparing for quantum-resistant security migration

---

## Expected Impact

QuantumShield AI aims to increase awareness of quantum security threats and help users take proactive actions before quantum computers become a practical threat.

By combining AI and PQC technologies, the platform provides an accessible approach for understanding and improving future cybersecurity readiness.
